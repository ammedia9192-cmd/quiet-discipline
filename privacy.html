Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the "Quiet Discipline" content service and its software (the "Service"), operated by the Quiet Discipline creator ("we", "us", "our"), handles information. We built the Service to publish our own videos to our own social media accounts.

1. Information we access

The Service uses the official APIs of YouTube, Meta (Instagram and Facebook), and TikTok to publish content to accounts we own. To do this we hold access tokens that authorize posting on our behalf. These tokens are stored privately and are not shared.

2. Information we do NOT collect

The Service is not offered to the public and does not have external users. We do not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from viewers of our videos. We do not run advertising trackers or profile individuals. Any analytics we see are the standard aggregate metrics provided by the platforms themselves (such as views and watch time) about our own posts.

3. How we use API access

API access is used solely to: create and upload videos, set titles, descriptions, captions, and thumbnails, and read back the resulting post links and basic performance metrics for our own accounts.

4. Data storage and security

Access tokens and configuration are stored in a private environment and are not transmitted to any third party other than the respective platform APIs they authenticate with. We take reasonable measures to protect this information.

5. Third-party platforms

Our use of platform data complies with the developer policies of YouTube, Meta, and TikTok. Information handled through those platforms is also governed by their respective privacy policies.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain access tokens only for as long as needed to operate the Service and revoke them when no longer required. To request that any information associated with the Service be deleted, contact us at the address below.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under the age of 13.

8. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time; the "last updated" date above reflects the latest revision.
